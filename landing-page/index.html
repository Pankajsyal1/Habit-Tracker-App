About the App

Consistency made visible

Habit Tracker helps you build and maintain positive habits by providing clear, visual feedback on your progress. See your streaks grow, track completions on a beautiful calendar, and stay motivated with real-time analytics.

Available on both web and mobile, your habits sync seamlessly across devices. Whether it's daily meditation, reading, or exercise — we make it stick.

📱 Cross-Platform 🔥 Streak Tracking 📊 Analytics
